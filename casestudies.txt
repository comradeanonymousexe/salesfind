Econur needed a professional website and e-commerce presence, a structured CRM and sales tracking system, and a clear social media identity. The brand was in an early stage with around 10 monthly sales, mainly from personal networks, and needed a stronger path toward credibility and investment readiness.



The Solution
Salesfind stepped in as Econur’s full-service Marketing and IT partner, delivering:


E-commerce Landing Page

A clean, conversion-focused website showcasing Econur’s product varieties with a seamless WhatsApp-based ordering flow — designed specifically for the Bangladesh market.


Custom CRM System

A purpose-built CRM to capture and manage leads, track customer relationships, identify re-order opportunities, and systematically request reviews. Available at the client’s dedicated CRM portal.


Zero-Budget Growth Campaign

An organic-first marketing strategy built on micro-influencer outreach, organic social engagement, and a referral loop — engineered for maximum impact at near-zero ad spend, designed specifically for a bootstrapped founder.

Trust-Signal Website

Digital presentation of their organizational profile, regulatory credentials (NGO Affairs Bureau, Social Welfare, TIN), leadership teams, and 22+ global donor partnerships — creating a coherent, professional, and compliant public face for the organization.

Client Background
J One Lab Studio is a creative education hub dedicated to training the next generation of video editors, motion designers, and post-production professionals.

Interactive EdTech Platform & LMS

A fully integrated learning ecosystem designed for seamless student experiences — featuring intuitive course navigation, secure video hosting architecture, dedicated user dashboards, and streamlined checkout flows for new enrollments.

Creative Brand & Portfolio Presentation

A visually striking website aesthetic aligned with the dynamic, fast-paced world of video editing. The platform is structured to showcase course roadmaps, instructor expertise, and compelling student showcases to build credibility and drive conversions.

Results & Impact
Custom LMS infrastructure and learning workflows currently in active deployment
Transitioning fragmented learning materials into a unified, professional online academy
