Privacy Policy

Salesfind Marketing and IT ("Salesfind", "we", "us" or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it and the choices you have, whether you visit salesfind.org, contact us, or work with us as a client.

1. Who We Are & Scope

Salesfind Marketing and IT is a digital marketing and IT services business based in Akhaliya, Sylhet, Bangladesh. We serve clients in Bangladesh and internationally. For personal data we collect about our own website visitors, clients and enquirers, we are the "data controller".

This policy covers our website, our communications with you (WhatsApp, email, phone and video calls), and the services we deliver: B2B lead generation, email marketing and cold outreach, web development, SEO, branding and design, CRM and web applications, and IT solutions and training.

2. Information We Collect

Information you give us. When you enquire about or buy our services, we may collect:

  • Your name, business name, job title, email address, phone and WhatsApp number
  • Project details, business goals, brand assets, content and files you send us
  • Access credentials you choose to grant (e.g. hosting, CMS, domain/DNS, ad accounts, CRM, email platforms) so we can do the agreed work
  • Billing details needed to invoice you and confirm payments

Information collected automatically. When you visit our website, we and our service providers may collect technical data such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages viewed and referring site. See Section 5.

Information from public and third-party sources. To deliver B2B lead generation services, we collect business contact information, such as names, job titles, work email addresses, company names and company details. We get this from publicly available sources (company websites, professional networks, business directories) and reputable data providers.

We never ask for or store full card numbers. Payments made through bKash, Nagad, Rocket, bank transfer, Wise or Payoneer are handled by those providers under their own privacy policies.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to enquiries, prepare proposals and communicate with you
  • To deliver, manage and support the services you have engaged us for
  • To invoice you, process payments and keep business and tax records
  • To build prospect lists and run outreach campaigns on behalf of our clients
  • To improve our website, services and client experience
  • To protect our business, prevent fraud and comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. Client Data & B2B Prospect Data

Data we handle for clients. When we access or process personal data that belongs to a client (for example their customer database, CRM records or website users), we act on the client's behalf and only as instructed. The client remains responsible for having a lawful basis to collect and use that data.

If we contacted you as a prospect. If you got a business email or message from us or for one of our clients, your business contact details were taken from public or reputable third-party sources. We rely on legitimate business interest to process them. You can opt out at any time by replying "unsubscribe" or emailing us. We will add you to a suppression list so you are not contacted again.

Want your details removed from our prospect data? Email salesfindofficial@gmail.com with the subject "Remove my data".

5. Cookies & Analytics

We do not use advertising cookies, retargeting pixels or social media tracking pixels on our website.

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand overall traffic, such as how many people visit, which pages they view and roughly where they come from. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies, and Google processes this data under its own privacy policy. You can opt out with the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on or by blocking cookies in your browser.

Our website also loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers and shares your IP address with them.

6. How We Share Information

We only share personal information when it is needed, with:

  • Service providers that help us run our business, such as email and cloud storage, WhatsApp, website hosting and payment providers
  • Team members and vetted contractors who need it to deliver your project and are bound by confidentiality
  • Our clients, when we deliver prospect lists or campaign results we were hired to produce
  • Authorities, when the law requires it or to protect our legal rights
  • A successor business, if Salesfind is reorganised, merged or sold

7. International Data Transfers

We are based in Bangladesh, and our service providers may process data in other countries, including the United States and the European Union. When personal data from the EU, UK or other regions is moved across borders, we take reasonable steps to keep it protected. Where available, this includes relying on our providers' standard contractual clauses.

8. Data Retention

We keep personal information only for as long as we need it for the purposes in this policy:

  • Client and project records: for the length of our engagement and up to 3 years afterwards, unless the law requires us to keep them longer (e.g. for tax or accounting)
  • Access credentials: deleted or handed back when a project or retainer ends. We recommend you change shared passwords at that point.
  • Enquiries that don't become projects: up to 12 months
  • Prospect data: for as long as the relevant campaign needs it, or until you ask us to delete it. Opt-out records are kept on a suppression list to honour your request.

9. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including access controls, secure credential handling and limiting access to people who need it. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Please share passwords through a secure method (e.g. a password manager share link or a temporary account), not in plain-text messages, wherever possible.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Ask us to delete your information
  • Object to or restrict certain processing, including direct marketing
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent
  • Get a copy of your data in a portable format

EU / UK residents (GDPR & UK GDPR). You also have the right to complain to your local data protection supervisory authority.

California residents (CCPA / CPRA). You have the right to know what personal information we collect, use and disclose; to delete it; to correct it; and to opt out of its "sale" or "sharing". We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights. You may use an authorised agent to make a request on your behalf.

To use any of these rights, email us at salesfindofficial@gmail.com. We may need to confirm your identity first. We aim to respond within 30 days, or within the time required by applicable law.

11. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are for businesses and professionals. They are not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us their information, contact us and we will delete it.

Our website links to third-party sites and services, such as WhatsApp, LinkedIn, X, Facebook and client websites in our case studies. We are not responsible for their privacy practices, so please read their policies.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last Updated" date at the top of this section. If the changes are significant, we will let active clients know.
